# Consolidated Report Template

Used in Phase 7 (Final Consolidation). Save output to `research-final-{topic-slug}.md`.

## Required Structure

```markdown
# Final Research Report: {research_topic}
*Consolidated from {N} sessions — {date}*

## Research Overview
- **Sessions:** {N} | **Sources:** {N} | **Period covered:** {earliest date} – {latest date}
- **Mode:** {research_mode} | **Geographic focus:** {geographic_focus}

## Master Timeline
- {date}: {event} [Source: {citation}]
...

## Key People
- {name} ({role}): {summary across sessions}

## Key Places
- {location}: {summary across sessions}

## Cross-Session Themes
- {theme}: {description of pattern visible across sessions}

## Integrated Narrative
{≥5 paragraph comprehensive account with inline citations}

## Established vs. Uncertain
**Established (confirmed by ≥2 independent sources):**
- {fact} [Sources: {citations}]

**Probable (single-source, no contradiction):**
- {finding} [Source: {citation}]

**Uncertain or Contradicted:**
- {claim} — {source A} says {X}, {source B} says {Y}

## Complete Bibliography
1. {newspaper}, {date}, p.{page} - "{headline}" URL: {url}
...

## Residual Gaps
- {question not resolved across all sessions}
...
```

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] All sessions from `journal_file` read and merged
- [ ] No duplicate timeline entries or bibliography items
- [ ] Every "Established" item has ≥2 genuinely independent sources
- [ ] All cross-session contradictions resolved or placed in Uncertain section
- [ ] Integrated narrative contains no claims absent from session findings
- [ ] Consolidation review-fix cycle(s) completed
